# Code of conduct

Be respectful and constructive in issues, pull requests, reviews and discussions.
Keep criticism about the work. Harassment, threats, discriminatory language,
sexual harassment and disclosure of another person's private information are
not acceptable.

Report abuse through GitHub's Report content option or privately to
**danielep71@gmail.com**. Do not describe an incident in a public issue.
Daniele Penza (@danielep71) handles reports confidentially, shares information
only as needed to investigate, and may remove content or restrict participation.
